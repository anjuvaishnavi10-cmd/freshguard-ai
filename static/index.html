How FreshGuard Works

FreshGuard uses separate AI agents to check future sales, product expiry and stock levels. A decision-making component compares possible actions and recommends the most suitable one. The store manager stays in control and approves or rejects every action.

1. Demand Agent — Predict future sales Predict future sales based on past customer purchases and daily shopping patterns.
2. Freshness Agent — Risk of expiring Tracks remaining shelf life and detects risk of expiring before stock can sell.
3. Action Agent — Suggested discount & Move stock Calculates the best suggested discount, when to move stock to another store, or when to restock.
4. AI decision maker Compares possible choices, resolves conflicts, and shows how sure the AI is.

How to configure Google OAuth in 4 steps:
  1. Go to Google Cloud Console → Credentials.
  2. Click Create Credentials ➔ OAuth client ID (Application Type: Web application).
  3. Under Authorized JavaScript origins, add: http://localhost:8080
  4. Copy your Client ID and paste it into the field below, then click Save.
